First release.

- `SteadyClick.exe`: Windows 10 and 11. Run it; nothing to install.
- `SteadyClick-mac.zip`: macOS 13 or newer, Apple silicon and Intel. Not notarized, so the first
  launch needs **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway**, and macOS will ask for
  Accessibility permission before it lets the app click.

See the [README](https://github.com/Zlerp/steadyclick#readme) for setup and what every setting does.
